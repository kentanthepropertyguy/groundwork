What are you buying?
New EC means bought from the developer. A resale EC is bought from its owner and follows different rules.

An HDB flat follows different rules, so no payment timeline is worked out for it here. It has its own loan rules (an HDB loan or a bank loan) and a 30% limit on the loan repayment against your income (MSR).

More detail about CPF OA you plan to use

CPF cannot pay the booking fee, the minimum cash or stamp duty up front. Your CPF housing limits may cap what you can use further, so the planner may show more CPF than you can actually use.

You and your loan

More detail about Monthly fixed income, both borrowers

It sets the loan a bank could lend, and checks a new EC against the household income ceiling. Stays on this device.

More detail about Borrower’s age

How much can I borrow? works it out and shows both ways of weighting it. The age sets the loan limit together with the tenure. Which income MAS weights by is not confirmed.

More detail about How much would you like to borrow?

Type an amount to borrow less. A smaller loan means more cash and CPF now and a smaller instalment. An amount above the estimated limit is not used: the result shows the limit that applies.

When did the EC site’s land tender close?
It sets the household income ceiling for a new EC. A balance unit in an older EC project keeps the lower ceiling, so choose “Before 24 Aug 2026” for it.
More detail about When did the EC site’s land tender close?

The ceiling is $16,000 a month if the tender closed before 24 Aug 2026, $18,000 if on or after. The developer or HDB can tell you.

Has the EC’s minimum occupation period (MOP) ended?
If you are not sure, the planner marks the financing as incomplete rather than guessing.
More detail about Has the EC’s minimum occupation period (MOP) ended?

It decides which income limits the bank applies to the loan. The owner’s agent or HDB can tell you.

Special cases (not confirmed)

MAS may leave the loan out when the sale is already contracted, or when you have signed an undertaking to HDB to sell. We have not confirmed these exclusions against the Notice, so we still count the loan as owed unless your bank confirms in writing.
