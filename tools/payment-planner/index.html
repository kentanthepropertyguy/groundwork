Your purchase

What are you buying?
New EC means bought from the developer. A resale EC is bought from its owner and follows different rules.
When did the EC site’s land tender close?
It sets the household income ceiling for a new EC: $16,000 a month if the tender closed before 24 Aug 2026, $18,000 if on or after. A balance unit in an older EC project keeps the lower ceiling, so choose “Before 24 Aug 2026” for it. The developer or HDB can tell you.
Has the EC’s minimum occupation period (MOP) ended?
It decides which income limits the bank applies to the loan. The owner’s agent or HDB can tell you. If you are not sure, the planner marks the financing as incomplete rather than guessing.
