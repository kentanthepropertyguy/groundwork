Privacy

What this site records, what stays on your device, and how to reach Ken.

Analytics and advertising: Google Analytics and Meta Pixel

On tools.kentanthepropertyguy.com, Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel record visits and which pages, tools, buttons and links are used, including links to other websites. They set cookies in your browser: _ga and _ga_PGTYDENS24 (Google Analytics, kept up to 2 years), _fbp (Meta, kept up to 90 days) and, if you arrive by tapping a Meta (Facebook or Instagram) ad, _fbc (Meta, kept up to 90 days). Meta’s script also keeps a note of the site you came from in your browser’s local storage (lastExternalReferrer and lastExternalReferrerTime, and multiFbc after an ad tap).

We use these analytics to understand which pages and tools visitors find useful, measure website performance, and improve the information and services provided. Ken also uses the Meta Pixel to measure his Facebook and Instagram ads and to show relevant ads to people who have visited this site (retargeting).

The events this site sends itself name the page, the tool, the step and the options you pick from lists (such as the type of home or flat), the development you look at, and simple counts such as how many past sales were found. These events never include amounts (prices, incomes, loans, CPF, cash, debts or budgets), budget ranges or whether a budget is within range, your block, street or other address, or anything you type.

Meta also detects some actions automatically, such as an enquiry. To do this it may analyse page content, button labels and how you interact with the page, and this can include a budget or other figures shown on the page at the time. Meta’s automatic matching also looks for contact details, such as an email address or phone number, in fields on a page; this site does not ask for them.

Google and Meta process what they receive under their own privacy policies (Google, Meta). They may combine it with other information they hold and process it outside Singapore. You can delete or block these cookies and this local storage in your browser settings, and manage the ads you see in your Google and Meta account settings.

Figures you type are kept in your browser

The calculators, the budget pages and the price checks work in your browser. To fill in the next step for you, figures are kept in this browser tab’s session storage. Groundwork does not send them to analytics or to Ken, and they are not put in the page address. Figures shown on a page can still be read by Meta’s automatic detection, described above.

Closing the tab normally clears them; a browser’s “reopen closed tab” or “duplicate tab” can bring them back. To remove them at once, choose “Clear my figures” in the Menu, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

WhatsApp

Nothing is sent to Ken unless you tap a WhatsApp button. WhatsApp then opens with a prepared message that you can read and change before you send it. Some messages include figures you typed, so read it first. WhatsApp (Meta) handles messages under its own terms.

No forms or accounts

There are no forms, sign-ups or accounts on this site.
