Upgrading from an HDB flat · what to work out, in order

Start with your planning range, then see what it buys.

The useful first number is what you could afford for a private home if you sell your flat first, and what is limiting it. With that range, the market becomes a short list rather than a long one.

1

Work out your planning range

From your flat’s value, loan, income and age: a planning range, the monthly instalment, and whether income, cash or the loan rules are the limit. It also explains what changes if you buy before you sell.
