Research a project

Understand a development, then compare it with another.

Search any Singapore private condo to see what its URA transactions say, then compare it with a second development and get a clear side-by-side read. Browse every development.

  1. Search for a development by name.
  2. See its resale and new-sale transactions: typical prices per square foot, sizes, and how they have moved.
  3. Choose “Compare a different project” to get a side-by-side read that matches the same sale type, size band and time window.

Every figure comes from URA private residential transaction records. Where the evidence is thin, the page says so instead of drawing a conclusion. It describes historical transactions and is not personalised advice, a valuation or a forecast.
